Help signing in

Confirmation emails, reset codes and account access in the Android app.

Forgot your password?

  1. Open the app’s sign-in screen and choose Help signing in → Reset password.
  2. Enter your registered email and request a reset code. Check your inbox and spam folder.
  3. Enter the code from the latest reset email in the app, choose a new password, then return to sign in.

If a request times out while setting a password, try signing in with the new password before starting another reset. Never email a password, reset code or recovery link to support.

I haven’t received an account-confirmation email

Check the spelling of your email and your spam folder. Enter the account email on the app’s sign-in screen, then use Help signing in → Resend confirmation email. Use the latest message. If messages still do not arrive, contact support with the attempt time and app version.

What happens after I confirm my email?

The confirmation link opens the Europe Drive Check account-confirmation page. Then open the Android app and sign in using the account email and password. A direct visit to the confirmation page does not itself confirm an account.

The link says expired or already used

First try signing in: the email may already be confirmed. If the app still asks for confirmation, request a new confirmation message. Use the most recent email. Older emails can contain an older redirect address.

My email or password is not accepted

Check that you are using the registered email. Use “Show password” only when nobody else can see your screen. If you do not remember the password, use the reset-code process above. A deleted account cannot sign in.

The app says it could not connect

Check your connection and try again. If the problem continues, email the exact message and app version to support. Remove personal details from screenshots.

What does “Keep me signed in” do?

This option stores protected sign-in information on this device without saving your password. Use it on your own device and sign out before lending the phone to somebody else.
