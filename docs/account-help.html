Help signing in

Find the next step for the problem you are seeing.

Forgot your password?

Self-service password recovery is not available on this preview. Contact support for help accessing your account.

Email sign-in support ↗

Do not email your password or verification code.

My email or password is not accepted

Check that you are using the email registered in the app. Use “Show password” only when nobody else can see your screen. Avoid repeated guesses; request help if you cannot sign in.

I haven’t received an account-confirmation email

Check your spam folder and the spelling of your email address. Use the most recent confirmation email. If it still does not arrive, contact support with the time of your attempt—do not send a password.

The app says it could not connect

Check your connection and try once more. If the problem continues, include the exact message and app version in your support email. Keep your existing installation while the issue is investigated.

What does “Keep me signed in” do?

This option keeps you signed in on your device without saving your password. Use it only on your own device. Sign out before lending the phone to somebody else.
