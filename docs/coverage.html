EUROPE DRIVE CHECK

Understand your trip check

Use the result as a planning aid and review the requirements that remain unresolved.

Coverage is limited

Europe Drive Check currently checks selected European road charges and access rules. A result does not confirm that every road, city restriction or driving requirement has been checked.

Read the action labels

Review before driving
Information needs confirmation or a requirement could not be resolved. Check the official source before travelling.
Payment or pass needed
The result identifies a road charge or pass for the selected route and vehicle. Confirm the current product, vehicle class, direction and validity before buying.
No action for this check
No action is required for that specific checked rule. It does not describe every requirement on your trip.

Known charges are not a complete trip budget

The displayed total can exclude unresolved requirements, fuel, parking and other costs. Purchases and payments happen outside the app.

An official website is blocked or won’t load

The destination website may reject or fail to load a request. Check the displayed domain and try the authority’s official homepage. Do not treat a failed link as confirmation that a charge or restriction does not apply. Report the link and error to support.

Check your inputs before travelling

Confirm the country in each place result, your vehicle details, and local dates and times at each end. Traffic, breaks and border delays can increase travel time.
