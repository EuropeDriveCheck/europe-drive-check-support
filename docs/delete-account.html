EUROPE DRIVE CHECK

Delete your account

Request deletion of your Europe Drive Check account without reinstalling the app.

Start with an email

Send your request from the email address registered with Europe Drive Check.

To
europedrivecheck.support@gmail.com
Subject
Europe Drive Check account deletion
Message
Please delete my Europe Drive Check account and its associated personal data.
Open deletion-request email ↗

This opens your email app. Review and send the message there; clicking the button does not submit or complete deletion.

What happens next?

  1. Verify account ownership. The developer may ask you to confirm control of the account’s registered email. Never send your password or a recovery code.
  2. Review the data linked to your account. The request covers your account and the personal data associated with it.
  3. Receive a completion reply. The developer will explain when the request is complete and whether any data must be retained, why, and for how long.

Can’t access your registered email?

Contact support and explain the problem. Ownership must be verified before an account can be deleted. Do not send identity documents unless the developer explains a necessary, secure verification process.

Deletion and uninstalling are different

Uninstalling removes the app from your phone; it does not itself delete your server account. Signing out also does not delete the account.

Draft instructions: processing times and any retention exceptions are still being finalised. Sending an email does not itself delete your account.
