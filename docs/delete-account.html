Delete your account

Request removal of your Europe Drive Check account and associated app-held personal data.

Send a deletion request

You can request deletion here even if you cannot sign in or have uninstalled the app. Send the request from the email address registered with Europe Drive Check.

To
europedrivecheck.support@gmail.com
Subject
Europe Drive Check account deletion
Message
Please delete my Europe Drive Check account and its associated personal data.
Open deletion-request email ↗

Review and send the message in your email app. Opening this draft does not send the request or delete your account. If no email app opens, use the address and subject above in your usual email service.

Request from the Android app

Open Account & help → Request account deletion → Open email, then review and send the draft from your registered address. This uses the same support process.

What happens next?

  1. Ownership check. We verify control of the registered email and ask for any necessary clarification. Never send your password, a confirmation link or recovery code.
  2. Delete the account and associated app-held data. We remove the sign-in account and identity, refresh sessions, any account-linked trip/report records, and account-keyed request counters. Removal cannot be undone.
  3. Completion reply. We confirm when deletion is complete and explain any specific information that has to remain, the reason and the applicable retention period.

Processing time

We aim to acknowledge your request within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days of receiving it. If ownership cannot yet be verified or an exception applies, we explain the next steps and expected timing within that period. Any shorter deadline required by applicable law still applies.

What may remain temporarily?

A previously issued short-lived access token may remain valid until it expires; account deletion is not a promise of instant invalidation of every open session. The deleted account cannot create a new sign-in or refresh session.

Provider-held operational/security logs and backup copies may follow separate retention arrangements. Resolved support and deletion correspondence and routine request records are removed within 90 days of closure, as described in our privacy policy. If a specific record must be retained for a legal obligation or dispute, we explain why and for how long. The current app does not offer saved trip history.

Can’t access your registered email?

Contact support and explain the problem. We need to verify ownership before deleting an account. Do not send identity documents unless a necessary and secure verification process has been explained to you.

Uninstalling and signing out

Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete your server account. Sending the deletion email starts a manual request; deletion completes after verification and operator processing.
