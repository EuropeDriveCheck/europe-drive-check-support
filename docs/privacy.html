EUROPE DRIVE CHECK

Privacy notice — draft

An explanation of the current Android test build. Reviewed 19 October 2026.

This notice is a draft for the test app. The final provider disclosures, retention periods and deletion arrangements are still being completed. It is not the final public-release privacy policy.

Who operates the app?

Europe Drive Check is operated by Chandra Vijay Ratre. For privacy questions or requests, email europedrivecheck.support@gmail.com.

Information used by the app

Account information
Your email and password are used for account creation and sign-in. The app does not save your password on your device.
Trip details
The places, dates, times and vehicle details you enter are processed to provide your requested trip check.
Place searches
The text you submit is processed to find places. Use city or place names and avoid private or confidential addresses.
Technical information
Network information, including IP addresses and request information, may be processed to operate and protect the service. Account-linked usage information is used to prevent abuse.
Support requests
If you contact support, your email address, message and any attachments are used to respond to your request.

Why the information is used

Information is used to provide accounts and trip checks, protect the service, resolve problems and respond to account or privacy requests.

Information on your device

If you select “Keep me signed in”, the app stores your email and protected sign-in information on your device. Signing out clears this saved sign-in. The app may temporarily restore a trip form when you return to it.

The current test app does not request access to GPS location, contacts, the camera or microphone. It does not include advertising or analytics services.

Service providers and other websites

Supabase provides account and application hosting services. Geographic-data services process place searches and route requests. The test app credits LocationIQ and OpenStreetMap; the final provider list will be confirmed in the completed notice. Google Gmail processes messages sent to support.

GitHub Pages, the intended website host, records visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s information about Pages. This preview does not use advertising or analytics scripts.

Official guidance and purchase links open separate websites with their own privacy practices. Payments are made outside this app.

Storage and retention

Account information is retained until the account is deleted. Service usage information and support correspondence may also be retained. The detailed retention periods and any exceptions are still being finalised and will be included in the completed notice.

Your choices and requests

You can choose what trip details to submit, leave saved sign-in switched off, and contact support about access, correction or deletion of your personal data. See account deletion instructions. Do not send your password or verification codes to support.

Updates to this notice

This notice will be updated before public release. The app will link to the current published notice.
