EUROPE DRIVE CHECK

Privacy policy

How Europe Drive Check handles account, trip-check and support information.

Reviewed 24 October 2026. This notice covers the current Europe Drive Check Android app and support website. The app is currently in private testing.

Who is responsible?

Europe Drive Check is operated by Chandra Vijay Ratre, who is responsible for the personal data handled for the app. For privacy questions and requests, email europedrivecheck.support@gmail.com.

Information we use

Account and sign-in
Your email address, account identifier and authentication information are processed by Supabase to create and secure your account, confirm your email, sign you in and reset your password. The app sends passwords over HTTPS and does not save your password on the device.
Trip checks and place searches
We process the search text you submit, your selected origin and destination names and coordinates, local travel dates and times, and the vehicle details you enter, such as registration country, permitted mass, ownership, fuel and emissions or toll class. Use city or place names and avoid private or confidential addresses.
Service and security information
Hosting providers may process IP addresses, request and device/browser metadata, timestamps and diagnostic logs. The app’s backend keeps account-linked request counts and timestamps to limit abuse.
Support and deletion requests
We process your sender email, message, attachments and the correspondence needed to verify ownership and resolve your request. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or recovery links.
Sharing you choose
“Share this trip summary” sends the displayed route and date details to the app or recipient you choose through Android. That destination handles the shared copy under its own practices.

Why we use this information

We use it to provide the account and trip check you request, find places and routes, calculate supported road-charge and access checks, protect the service, troubleshoot problems and respond to support or privacy requests. We do not sell your personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, providing the requested account and trip service is necessary to perform that service; service protection and handling support requests rely on our legitimate interests, subject to your rights. We may also process limited information to meet legal obligations. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent.

Information on your device

If you select “Keep me signed in”, your email and refresh sign-in token are encrypted using Android Keystore and stored on that device. Signing out clears the app’s saved sign-in. Android may restore an interrupted trip form. The current app does not offer saved trip history; the displayed assessment is held for the current app session.

The current app requests internet access, but no device GPS, contacts, camera or microphone permission. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. This support site adds no advertising or analytics scripts, tracking cookies or account sign-in storage.

Who processes the information?

Supabase
Hosts authentication, backend processing, account records and security counters. See Supabase’s privacy policy.
LocationIQ (Unwired Labs)
Processes place-search text and origin/destination coordinates to return geographic results and routes. These requests go through our backend and do not include your account email or password. Map and road data include OpenStreetMap contributions. See LocationIQ’s privacy policy and data-processing information. During internal demo testing, Photon/komoot and the OSRM public demo may instead process search or route inputs; these demo services are not the supported public-release routing path.
Google Gmail
Processes account emails sent by the service and support/deletion correspondence. See Google’s privacy policy.
GitHub Pages
Hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Providers may process information in countries other than yours. Contact us for information about the providers and safeguards relevant to your data. Links to official guidance, purchase sites or other external services open sites with their own privacy practices. Payments take place outside this app.

How long information is kept

Account records
Kept while your account exists, then removed through the verified account-deletion process, along with any associated app-held trip/report records and account-keyed request counters.
Trip checks and search cache
The current trip-check path processes your inputs without writing saved trip history. Place-search responses may be held in server memory without an account email and reused for up to 15 minutes; entries are discarded when replaced or the server instance is cleared. This cache-reuse period does not set the retention of provider logs.
Abuse-prevention counters
A daily cleanup removes counters whose last update was more than 35 days ago. Continued activity can update that timestamp. Account-keyed counters are also removed during verified account deletion.
Support and deletion correspondence — proposed
We delete resolved support and deletion-request correspondence and routine request records within 90 days after the request is closed. A limited record may be retained longer only where needed for a specific legal obligation or dispute; we explain the reason and applicable period to the requester.
Provider logs, security records and backup copies
These follow the provider’s applicable service policies and retention arrangements. Account deletion does not immediately erase every provider log or backup copy. Where relevant to your request, we seek provider-side erasure where available or required and explain any records that must be retained and why. Contact us for the applicable details.

Your choices and rights

You can leave saved sign-in switched off, choose the trip information you submit, and request deletion without reinstalling the app. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, a copy or portability, restriction of processing, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also complain to your relevant data-protection authority. Email support to exercise a right; we may verify control of the account email before disclosing or deleting account information.

See the account-deletion page for the request steps, information removed and proposed processing time. The confirmation page strips sign-in tokens from its address and does not create a web sign-in session.

Security and changes

The app uses HTTPS for requests and protected device storage for optional saved sign-in. Access to operator tools is restricted. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee that a service is free of every security risk.

The current notice is published on this page with its review date. If our data practices change, we will update the notice to describe them.
